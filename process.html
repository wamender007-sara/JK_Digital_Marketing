The Systematic Growth Architecture

Zero Guesswork.
Scientific Marketing Execution.

We don't "run some ads and hope for the best." Our 5-stage proprietary growth framework has been honed across 45+ enterprise retainers to systematically turn ad spend into profitable, verified customer revenue.

01 Days 1 to 5

Growth Audit & Competitor Mining

Before shooting a single frame or spending a single rupee on ads, we perform an exhaustive teardown of your market landscape:

  • • Past Campaign Forensics: Auditing your past Meta & Google ad accounts to diagnose ad fatigue, high CPCs, or leaky targeting settings.
  • • Competitor Creative Intelligence: Reverse-engineering the top 5 competitors in your niche to identify their longest-running winning video hooks.
  • • Buyer Persona & Objection Mapping: Uncovering the exact 3 emotional triggers and 3 biggest skepticism points holding your prospects back.
02 Days 6 to 10

Creative Blueprint & Ad Scripting

Creative is the single largest variable in advertising performance. Our copywriters formulate conversion scripts engineered for retention:

  • • The 3-Second Hook Sprint: Writing 10+ distinct pattern-interrupt hooks per script (problem-first, controversial claim, visual intrigue, or founder question).
  • • Storyboarding & Shot List: Mapping out every shot, angle, lighting requirement, and B-roll element so shoot day operates with military precision.
  • • Direct-Response Copy & CTAs: Formulating headline angles, primary text variations, and irresistible lead magnets (e.g. Free Plant Walkthrough, Instant WhatsApp Quote).
03 Days 11 to 20

4K Production & Funnel Engineering

Production and web infrastructure are executed in tandem by our in-house Coimbatore creative and developer team:

  • • On-Location 4K Cinema Shoot: Multi-camera Sony FX3 / A7SIII setups, wireless audio, gimbal moves, and studio lighting at your premises.
  • • Retention Post-Production: Subtitle kinetic motion, dynamic sound design whooshes, color grading, and speed-ramped transitions.
  • • High-Speed Landing Page Build: Developing sub-second loading sales pages with full Meta Conversions API (CAPI), GTM, and GA4 tracking pixels.
04 Days 21 to 30

Multi-Channel Launch & Audience Mining

Campaigns go live under rigorous split-testing protocols to ensure ad spend is channeled only into validated winners:

  • • Creative Sandbox Testing: Testing 12+ creative combinations against Broad, Interest, and Custom Lookalike segments with micro-budgets.
  • • Bid Cap & CBO Automation: Moving validated winner ads into Advantage+ and Campaign Budget Optimization pools to control acquisition cost.
  • • Instant Lead Routing: Connecting form submissions directly into your sales team’s WhatsApp and CRM within 30 seconds of lead capture.
05 Day 31 & Ongoing

Algorithmic Scaling & Weekly Dashboards

Scale requires continuous creative rejuvenation to prevent ad fatigue and keep profitability compounding:

  • • Creative Refresh Cycles: Shooting fresh video hooks and seasonal ad variations every single month to sustain high CTRs.
  • • Live Looker Studio Dashboard: Real-time transparent metrics: Ad Spend, Leads Generated, Cost Per Acquisition, and Return on Ad Spend (ROAS).
  • • Weekly Executive Strategy Call: Founder-to-founder strategic reviews with K Indraajith to align on inventory, capacity, and next growth levers.

Sector 01 • Apparel, Fabrics & Export

Textile Mills, Knitwear & Fashion Brands

Based in Coimbatore with direct access to Tirupur, Erode, and Karur, we understand the textile lifeblood of Tamil Nadu. Whether you are transitioning from low-margin B2B garment export to higher-margin domestic D2C, or seeking bulk international sourcing buyers, we engineer visual storytelling that wins orders.

Commercial Model Shoots & E-Commerce Lookbooks:

Studio lighting, high-end fabric texture macros, and styled on-model shoots ready for Myntra, Amazon, and Shopify catalogs.

Meta Ads Direct-to-Consumer (D2C) Scaling:

High-retention reel ads showcasing fit, styling, and organic cotton certifications driving 4.5x+ ROAS on online purchases.

Sector 02 • B2B & Precision Engineering

Industrial Foundries, Pumps & CNC Machining

Coimbatore is the engineering capital of South India. Traditional industrial businesses often lose massive export contracts simply because their online presence looks like it was built in 2005. We elevate industrial manufacturers to global enterprise standards.

4K Cinematic Plant Tour & Machinery Films:

Showcasing factory floor capacity, ISO clean rooms, robotic CNC lines, and precision tolerance testing to instill immediate trust in overseas buyers.

High-Intent Google B2B Search Ads:

Capturing procurement directors searching specifically for high-capacity cast iron valves, submersible pumps, and custom CNC fabrication.

Sector 03 • Medical & Clinical Care

Hospitals, Dental & Super-Specialty Clinics

Healthcare marketing demands deep clinical empathy, compliance, and unshakable trust. Patients do not respond to hype; they search for doctor credentials, patient recovery stories, hygienic infrastructure, and clear treatment explanations.

Doctor Educational Reels & Patient Recovery Stories:

Doctors answering common surgical or treatment fears on camera, demystifying procedures and establishing immediate bedside manner before the patient even visits.

Google Maps Local #1 Rank & WhatsApp Booking:

Ranking your clinic #1 for "best orthopedic clinic near me" and routing calls directly to your front-desk reception WhatsApp.

Sector 04 • Luxury Villas & Commercial Property

Real Estate Builders & Gated Communities

Real estate leads are notorious for low intent when run through lazy generic forms. We combine cinematic walkthrough videos, location lifestyle reels, and verified 2-step qualification funnels that ensure your sales team only calls buyers with actual capital and ready budgets.

Cinematic Drone & Walkthrough Video Production:

Aerial drone shots capturing project scale, proximity to IT corridors/airports, and model villa interiors that generate genuine emotional desire.

NRI & HNI Targeted Meta Ads:

Precision targeting of Tamil and South Indian diaspora in Dubai, Singapore, and Bangalore looking for premium retirement or investment plots.

Sector 05 • Direct-To-Consumer Commerce

E-Commerce Brands & Consumer Packaged Goods

With rising customer acquisition costs (CAC), e-commerce brands cannot survive on standard image ads alone. We implement creative sprint testing, generating dozens of UGC-style video hooks, dynamic catalog ads, and post-purchase SMS/WhatsApp flows to maximize Customer Lifetime Value (LTV).

Viral Short-Form Hook Testing:

Testing 15+ different opening 3-second hooks each month to identify winner creatives before pouring ad spend.

Abandoned Cart & Retargeting Funnels:

Customized dynamic retargeting recovering 25%+ of lost checkouts through customer reviews and limited-time offer triggers.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Pricing & Retainer FAQs

Is ad spend included in the monthly retainer?

No. Ad spend is paid directly by your company to Meta (Facebook/Instagram) or Google via your own billing profile. This ensures you maintain 100% ownership of your ad accounts and historical data.

Are there long-term lock-in contracts?

We work on straightforward 3-month initial agreements to allow algorithms and A/B test variations to mature. After that, retainers convert to month-to-month with a 30-day notice.

Who manages my campaigns day-to-day?

Founder & CEO K Indraajith personally oversees strategic creative direction and media buying optimization, supported by our in-house Coimbatore videographers and developers.

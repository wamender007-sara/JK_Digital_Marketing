# JK Digital Marketing

> **Grow Your Brand Digitally**

A modern, responsive, agency-style single-page marketing website for **JK Digital Marketing**, built with clean semantic HTML, Tailwind CSS, custom SVG iconography, and responsive interactions.

## 🚀 Overview

- **Brand:** JK Digital Marketing
- **Tagline:** Grow Your Brand Digitally / Strategy. Brand. Growth.
- **Location:** Eachanari, Coimbatore – 641021, Tamil Nadu, India
- **Founder & CEO:** K Indraajith

## 🌟 Features

- **Sticky Navigation Bar:** Stylized crimson "JK" mark, desktop navigation, responsive mobile menu drawer, and quick consultation CTA.
- **Hero Section:** High-converting layout with custom laptop mockup displaying rising growth telemetry, floating authentic social platform badges (Instagram, LinkedIn, Facebook, Google, YouTube, WhatsApp, Meta, X), and trust badges.
- **Core Services:** End-to-end digital solutions (SEO, Meta Ads, Google Ads, Video Editing, Content Creation, Web Development) with hover lift interactions.
- **About Us & Why Choose Us:** Storytelling narrative, live animated stats counter (50+ Happy Clients, 100+ Projects Completed, 4.9 Rating), and value proposition checklist.
- **Client Testimonials:** Social proof cards with star ratings and responsive mobile swipe carousel.
- **Interactive Free Quote Modal:** Full consultation request modal with backdrop blur and direct WhatsApp auto-fill link.
- **Comprehensive Footer:** Quick links, service breakdown, social channels, and legal policies.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- **HTML5 & Vanilla JavaScript**
- **Tailwind CSS** (via CDN with custom crimson & blush theme configuration)
- **Google Fonts:** [Poppins](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Poppins)
- **Pure Vector SVGs** for brand marks and social icons

## 💻 Local Development

To run the local preview server:

```bash
node server.js
```

Then open your browser at `http://localhost:3000/`.

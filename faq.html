Frequently Asked Questions
& Strategic Client Guide.

Everything you need to know about partnering with JK Digital Marketing: contracts, ad spend billing, creative asset ownership, timelines, and guarantees.

01. Who pays the ad spend on Meta and Google, and how is it billed? +

You pay Meta and Google directly. Your ad accounts are linked to your company credit card or debit card. JK Digital Marketing charges a transparent, flat monthly management and production retainer. We never take hidden markups or percentages on your ad spend, ensuring our advice remains 100% unbiased.

02. Who owns the ad accounts, video assets, and web source code? +

You own 100% of everything. All campaigns run inside your ad manager, all website code is handed over to your hosting, and all raw footage and edited 4K video exports belong entirely to you. If you ever pause or transition services, you retain all data, tracking pixels, and creative assets with zero vendor lock-in.

03. How fast can we expect verified leads or purchase orders? +

For paid advertising (Meta Ads & Google Search Ads), campaigns typically begin generating validated customer inquiries within 48 to 72 hours of launch. For organic SEO, Google Map Pack dominance, and brand positioning, measurable growth compounds over 60 to 90 days as domain trust solidifies.

04. Do you create ad creatives in both Tamil and English? +

Yes! Being native to Eachanari, Coimbatore, our copywriters, voiceover artists, and directors produce content in native Tamil, English, and bilingual "Tanglish". We also produce national campaigns in Hindi when scaling D2C brands across Northern and Western India.

05. Can we hire you for 4K video shoot and editing alone without ad management? +

Absolutely. While our 360° Retainer provides the highest ROI by connecting production directly to media buying, you can book our production crew for standalone corporate films, product lookbooks, model shoots, or reel batches as one-off projects.

06. How does video production logistics work for businesses outside Coimbatore? +

Our cinema production team regularly travels across Tirupur, Erode, Salem, Chennai, Bangalore, and Kerala. We bring all 4K cinema gear, wireless microphones, lighting equipment, and gimbal rigs right to your factory, showroom, hospital, or property site for seamless 1-day or 2-day shoot schedules.

07. How are incoming leads routed to our sales team? +

Speed to lead is critical. The moment a customer submits their information on your Meta ad or web landing page, our webhook automations push their name, verified phone number, city, and exact inquiry to your sales team’s WhatsApp group and Google Sheet in under 30 seconds.

08. What are the contract terms and cancellation policies? +

We believe client retention should be earned through performance, not restrictive legal handcuffs. Our standard retainer agreements operate on 3-month growth sprints with a straightforward 30-day notice period.
